# Svarah.AI — brag plan

**What it is:** a voice-first marketing tool for shopkeepers. Say your offer once; get a poster and ready-to-post captions for every channel, with every number checked against what you said.
**Angle:** one sentence in, a whole campaign out — shown as a single unbroken use of the real workspace.
**Hook:** the app's own opening question, full screen: "What are you offering today?"
**Tone:** default (clean, punchy), dark workspace theme with the lime accent; light landing-page palette for the outro.
**Identity:** #141513 paper, #d8f878 lime, Manrope / DM Sans / DM Mono — loaded from the project's own stylesheets.
**Source material:** real `voice-workspace.css` + markup mirroring `VoiceWorkspace.jsx`; facts, captions and poster produced by running the backend pipeline offline (mock mode, throwaway DB) on the test suite's sample offer.

| # | Time | Scene |
|---|------|-------|
| 1 | 0.0–2.4 | Hook: lime mark pops, "What are you offering today?" |
| 2 | 2.4–7.2 | Speak: hold the mic, live transcript types out the offer |
| 3 | 7.2–10.8 | Understand: "Here is what I understood" facts land one by one; tap "Looks right — make my posts" |
| 4 | 10.8–14.4 | Make: five steps tick off, ending on "Checking every number" |
| 5 | 14.4–19.2 | Campaign: poster + Instagram / WhatsApp / Facebook / X captions; "Every number matches your offer." |
| 6 | 19.2–22.4 | Outro: SVARAH.AI — "CREATE ONCE. PUBLISH EVERYWHERE." — Start Creating Free |

**Sound:** original track, D major, 100 BPM (one bar = 2.4s, every scene change lands on a bar line). Pad + marimba arpeggio, drums enter with the facts, riser through the "make" steps, lead melody on the campaign reveal, one resolved chord for the outro. Effects (mic on/off, fact bells, step ticks, confirmation chime) are pitched in key and on the beat grid.
